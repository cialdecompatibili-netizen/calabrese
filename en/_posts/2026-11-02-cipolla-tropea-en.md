The **Cipolla Rossa di Tropea Calabria** is a product with protected geographical indication (PGI). It is appreciated above all for its sweet flavour, which makes it suitable raw too.

## How to use it

- **Raw**, in thin slices in a salad or on a sandwich.
- **Sweet and sour**, slowly cooked with vinegar and sugar.
- **As a jam**, to go with cheese and meat.
- **Roasted**, whole or in wedges, with a drizzle of oil.

## How to store it

Onions should be kept dry, in a cool and airy place, without piling them up. Cut ones keep in the fridge for a few days.

For growing, varieties differ and sowing times depend on the climate: always follow the instructions on the seed packet.
