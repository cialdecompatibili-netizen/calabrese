Chilli is one of the symbols of Calabrian cooking, and at the end of the season you often pick more than you can eat fresh. Here are the simplest ways to preserve it.

## Whole dried

Pick fully ripe fruits and thread them on a string, to hang them in a dry, airy and bright place, but not in direct sunlight. When they are dry and brittle, put them in a closed jar.

## Ground

Well-dried chilli can be ground and kept in an airtight container, away from light and moisture. Use it by the pinch on pasta, meat and vegetables.

## In oil

Dried chilli can be left to infuse in extra virgin olive oil: the oil must be stored correctly and used quickly, because fresh chilli in oil may not be safe.

## A practical tip

Handle chilli with gloves and do not touch your eyes. Heat varies a lot from one variety to another: always taste with care.
